Who We Are

The Namibia Economic Observatory (NEO) is an independent economic research and data analytics platform dedicated to improving public access to reliable economic information. Through research, interactive dashboards, statistical analysis and data visualization, NEO transforms complex datasets into practical insights that support evidence-based decision-making across Namibia.

Our Mission

To make economic information accessible, understandable and useful for researchers, students, businesses, policymakers and the wider public by producing reliable research and innovative data solutions.

Our Vision

To become Namibia's leading digital economic observatory and a trusted source of economic intelligence for national development.

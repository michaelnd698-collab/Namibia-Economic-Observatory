About Us

Empowering Namibia with reliable economic insights.

The Namibia Economic Observatory (NEO) is an independent platform founded by Michael Ndiiwda Henok to transform Namibia's economic data into accessible research, interactive dashboards and evidence-based insights for students, researchers, businesses, policymakers and the public. Our mission is to promote informed decision-making by making reliable economic information easy to understand, explore and use.
